Your retention curve shows you where viewers left. Insights tells you what was on screen
when they left, and what to do about it.

Open a video and click **Insights**.

## When a report runs

A report runs automatically once a video passes **300 plays**, and re-runs at most once
every 24 hours after that. Insights is available on Basic plans and up.

You do not schedule it and you do not prompt it. If the video does not have enough
traffic yet, the tab tells you how many plays are still missing.

## What is in a report

Every report opens with one headline: the single change worth making first. Under it:

  </Card>
  <Card title="Dead zones" icon="scissors">

Every conclusion cites the numbered findings it came from, and every finding carries its
own evidence sentence with the measured numbers in it. If a claim does not have a number
behind it, it does not ship.

## What we checked

This is the panel that separates Insights from a wall of AI confidence: it shows you all
nine angles the report looked at, including the ones that found nothing and the ones that
do not have enough data yet.

Each of the nine dimensions draws its own wedge:

| Wedge | Reads as |
| --- | --- |
| Pale grey, wide | **Data depth.** How much evidence fed that dimension. |
| Solid coral, narrow | **Findings strength.** How far it reaches is the confidence of the strongest finding. How wide it is, is how many findings fired. |

Three states are worth telling apart, and the chart draws each one differently:

- **A grey wedge with coral inside it.** The dimension had data and found something.
- **A grey wedge with no coral.** The dimension had plenty of data and found nothing
  unusual. That is a real answer, not a gap.
- **No wedge at all.** The signal is not being collected on this video yet. The label is
  greyed out and the list on the right says what is still missing.

The list beside the chart says the same thing in words, one row per dimension, with the
volumes that fed it. When a check is still collecting, the row names the specific check
rather than the whole dimension: a dimension can have plenty of data overall while one of
its sub-checks is still waiting.

<Note>
  A dimension sitting at zero is normal and expected. Buyer behaviour needs conversions,
  Intent needs checkout clicks, and Page scroll needs the newer behaviour signals. A
  video can carry a strong report with four of the nine dimensions quiet.
</Note>

## Sound on versus muted

When your video autoplays muted, the report compares viewers who turned the sound on
against viewers who stayed muted, **within the autoplay population only**.

<Warning>
  **This comparison is observational. It does not mean turning the sound on causes the
  difference.** Browsers decide who gets unmuted autoplay from their own record of how
  much video that person already engages with, so the sound-on group is selected in
  advance for being the kind of viewer who watches. Read the gap as a description of two
  groups you already have, not as the lift you would get by forcing sound on everyone.
</Warning>

## Applying a suggestion

A timed CTA suggestion applies straight to the video's timed events with one click. It
arrives with the trigger time, the label and the show/hide selector already filled in, so
what you are approving is the moment, not a form.

Everything else in the report is advice you act on yourself: trims live in your editor,
settings patches point at the exact toggle, and test ideas name the hypothesis.

## The digest email

When a new report is ready, TrackPlay emails you a digest with the headline for each
video. It arrives in **the language set on your profile**, like every other TrackPlay
email.

Turn it off from the Insights tab. The reports keep generating; you just stop being
emailed about them.
