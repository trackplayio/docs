<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Click **Integrations** in the sidebar. Every platform is a card in the grid.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Find the card and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your tracking parameter">
    On the **Settings** tab, pick the parameter TrackPlay should use to carry the viewer's
    session through to the sale. Any unused one works. Confirm your affiliates are not
    already using it for something else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save & Verify">
    Click **Save & Verify**. The **Postback Setup** window opens and starts watching for a
    test conversion in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the postback URL">
    Copy the URL from that window into your cart's postback settings, then push a test
    sale through.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until a test conversion lands, the integration shows **Not configured**. Once one arrives,
it flips to **Active** by itself. You do not have to come back and confirm anything.
