Connect your ThriveCart account to TrackPlay to automatically track sales and inject session IDs into checkout links.

## Setup Instructions

### Step 1: Connect
1. Go to **Integrations** → find **ThriveCart** → click **Connect**

### Step 2: Configure
1. Select the tracking parameter (`affiliate_id`, `aff_sub`, or `utm_content`)
2. Click **Save & Verify**

### Step 3: Add Webhook and Verify
1. Copy the Webhook URL from the setup modal
2. In ThriveCart: go to **Product → Fulfillment → Webhook URL**
3. Paste the URL and save
4. Trigger a test order
5. TrackPlay detects the test and marks the integration **Active**

## How It Works
- All `thrivecart.com` links on your page are automatically updated with the session ID
- When a purchase is completed, ThriveCart sends a webhook to TrackPlay
- The sale is attributed to the video session that drove the conversion
