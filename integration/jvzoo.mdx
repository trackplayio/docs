Connect your JVZoo vendor account to TrackPlay to automatically track affiliate sales and inject session IDs into checkout links.

## Setup Instructions

### Step 1: Connect
1. Go to **Integrations** → find **JVZoo** → click **Connect**

### Step 2: Configure
1. Select the tracking parameter (`aff_sub`, `aff_sub2`, `aff_sub3`, `aff_sub4`, or `aff_sub5`)
2. Click **Save & Verify**

### Step 3: Add IPN URL and Verify
1. Copy the IPN URL from the setup modal
2. In JVZoo: go to **Vendor → Product → Advanced → IPN URL**
3. Paste the URL and save
4. Trigger a test purchase
5. TrackPlay detects the test and marks the integration **Active**

## How It Works
- All `jvzoo.com` links on your page are automatically updated with the session ID
- When a purchase is completed, JVZoo sends an IPN notification to TrackPlay
- The sale is attributed to the video session that drove the conversion
