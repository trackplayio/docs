RedTrack and Everflow attribute a sale to a click. TrackPlay fires a GET request to a URL
template you provide, with macros filled in from the conversion.

## Connect it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Integrations">
    Click **Integrations** in the sidebar, find **RedTrack** or **Everflow**, and click
    **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your postback URL">
    Use your tracker's macro syntax for anything TrackPlay does not fill in itself, and
    TrackPlay's macros (below) for the rest.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The integration goes **Active** immediately. There is no test postback to wait for:
    the next real conversion is the first delivery.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<CodeGroup>

```text RedTrack
https://yourdomain.rdtk.io/postback?clickid={clickid}&sum={payout}
```

```text Everflow
https://www.yourdomain.com/?nid=1&transaction_id={clickid}&amount={payout}
```

</CodeGroup>

## Macros

<ParamField body="{clickid}" type="string">
  The tracker's own click id for the converting session. See below: this only resolves
  for a session TrackPlay can trace back to a click.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="{payout}" type="string">
  The conversion value.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="{currency}" type="string">
  Three-letter currency code.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="{video_code}" type="string">
  The video the sale is credited to.
</ParamField>
<ParamField body="{order_id}" type="string">
  The cart's order id, or the `conversion_id` you sent if you used the Conversions API.
</ParamField>

## When the postback does not fire

<Warning>
  **Read this before you rely on these postbacks.**

  `{clickid}` is filled from one of three places, in order: a `clickid` on the conversion
  payload, a `click_id` on it, or a lookup against the session's captured click identity
  under the tracker's own parameter names (`rtkclickid`, `clickid`, `cid` for RedTrack;
  `ef_transaction_id`, `clickid`, `click_id` for Everflow).

  That third path does not resolve in practice. The player's click-id capture list holds
  ad-platform ids (`fbclid`, `gclid`, `ttclid` and similar) and **deliberately does not
  capture tracker click ids**, so there is nothing stored under any of the names the
  lookup asks for. Unless your conversion payload carries `clickid` or `click_id`
  directly, `{clickid}` resolves to empty.

  When it is empty, TrackPlay does **not** send the postback. That is deliberate: a
  tracker that receives an empty `clickid` records an unattributable conversion and its
  log still reads "delivered". Not sending it is the only way an empty value does not
  quietly corrupt your tracker's numbers.

  The practical consequence is that the integration can sit on **Active** and never
  deliver anything, and there is no customer-facing delivery log to tell you so. Confirm
  with your tracker that conversions are arriving before you trust these numbers.
</Warning>

A conversion with no session at all (a sale from a link you built by hand, or one
TrackPlay could not join to a play) never reaches this stage: RedTrack and Everflow
postbacks fire only for `conversion.created`, and only once a session is known.

## Fires on real revenue only

Like every server-side sender, RedTrack and Everflow only hear about a genuine `purchase`
or `rebill`. A refund, a chargeback, and anything flagged as a test sale never generate a
postback.

## When it does not work

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Integration shows Active but the tracker never logs a conversion">
    Confirm the postback URL is saved with the exact macro syntax above (case-sensitive
    curly braces). If the URL is correct, the most common cause is an unresolved
    `{clickid}`: see the warning above. TrackPlay does not surface a per-conversion delivery
    log for this integration today, so the fastest way to confirm is a real sale from
    traffic you know carries a trackable click id.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some conversions post, others silently do not">
    Expected if your traffic mix includes sessions with no resolvable click id alongside
    sessions that have one. Direct traffic, email clicks, and organic sessions never carry
    a click id and never fire a postback.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="RedTrack or Everflow rejects the postback">
    Check `{payout}` and `{currency}` are in the position your tracker's template expects,
    and that the base URL (your tracker domain, not TrackPlay's) is correct. TrackPlay logs
    the HTTP status it got back; a 4xx from your tracker means the request reached it but
    the tracker rejected the parameters.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
