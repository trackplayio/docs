Connect your WarriorPlus vendor account to TrackPlay to automatically track affiliate sales and inject session IDs into offer links.

## Setup Instructions

### Step 1: Connect
1. Go to **Integrations** → find **WarriorPlus** → click **Connect**

### Step 2: Configure
1. Select the tracking parameter: `sid` or `tid`
2. Click **Save & Verify**

<Note>
  `sid` and `tid` are the only two parameters WarriorPlus echoes back on the postback.
  Anything else means the sale posts back without a session and is credited as organic,
  with no error shown and the integration still reading **Active**.
</Note>

### Step 3: Add Postback URL and Verify
1. Copy the Postback URL from the setup modal
2. In WarriorPlus: go to **Vendor → Offer → Postback URL**
3. Paste the URL and save
4. Trigger a test sale using the WarriorPlus test tool
5. TrackPlay detects the test and marks the integration **Active**

## How It Works
- All `warriorplus.com` links on your page are automatically updated with the session ID
- When a sale is completed, WarriorPlus sends a postback to TrackPlay
- The sale is attributed to the video session that drove the conversion
